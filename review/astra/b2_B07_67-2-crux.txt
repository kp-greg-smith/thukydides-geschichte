1. **τὸ μέρος** is best taken adverbially: “in part,” or “for their share,” qualifying **βλάψωσιν**. “Interests” is an explanatory addition; “the part of the city” misrepresents the construction. Retain **τὸ μέρος** as transmitted, without making it an apposition to “city.”

2. **ἐκείνου refers to Sadocus**: “his city” is **Athens**, through his newly acquired citizenship. The Athenians appeal to his responsibility toward his own adopted city.

3. **A is closest**, provided “his” means Sadocus. I would print:

   “so that, by crossing over to the King, they might not contribute to harming his city [Athens].”
