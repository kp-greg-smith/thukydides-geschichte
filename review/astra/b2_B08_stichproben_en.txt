### 2.71.4 — Übersetzung (en):
And we take the gods to witness — the gods of the oaths that were sworn on that day, and your ancestral gods and our own gods of this country — and we say to you: do no wrong to the Plataean land, do not transgress the oaths, but suffer us to dwell autonomous, even as Pausanias judged it right.

### 2.72.3 — Griechisch:

### 2.72.3 — Übersetzung (en):
And he, heartening them upon these things, said: “But do you: hand over the city and the houses to us, to the Lacedaemonians, and show forth the boundaries of the land, and the trees that are your own, by number, and whatever else beside that can be brought to a number; and yourselves withdraw wheresoever you please, so long as the war is; and when it is past, we will give you back all that we take over. Until that time we will hold it as a deposit, working the land and bringing in a yield, such as we trust will suffice you.”

### 2.73.1 — Übersetzung (en):
And they, having heard it, went back again into the city, and, having taken counsel with the people, told him that they wished first to lay before the Athenians the things that he challenged, and — if they persuaded them — to do them; and until that time they bade him make a truce with them and not ravage the land. And he concluded a truce for the days within which the return was like to be made, and he did not cut up the land.
