Thucydides 2.13.1, transmitted Oxford/Jones. Two construction questions about one sentence.
Sentence part 1: "Περικλῆς ὁ Ξανθίππου στρατηγὸς ὢν Ἀθηναίων δέκατος αὐτός, ὡς ἔγνω τὴν ἐσβολὴν ἐσομένην..."
Three renderings of "στρατηγὸς ὢν Ἀθηναίων δέκατος αὐτός":
(i) "Pericles ... then general of the Athenians for the tenth time" (i.e. serving his tenth generalship),
(ii) "being the tenth general of the Athenians" (i.e. in the tenth year of the war / in some succession),
(iii) "being at that time one of the ten generals of the Athenians" (i.e. a member of the board of ten).
Question 1: which reading is standard for "δέκατος αὐτός" here? Note: at Thucydides 1.61.1 the transmitted "πέμπτον αὐτὸν στρατηγόν" was taken as "himself the fifth" (= serving his fifth term); does the same idiom govern here?

Sentence part 2: "ἢ καὶ Λακεδαιμονίων κελευσάντων ἐπὶ διαβολῇ τῇ ἑαυτοῦ γένηται τοῦτο, ὥσπερ καὶ τὰ ἄγη ἐλαύνειν προεῖπον ἕνεκα ἐκείνου, προηγόρευε τοῖς Ἀθηναίοις..."
Context: Pericles fears Archidamus might spare Pericles' own estates to embarrass him, "just as they had before given out that they would drive off ___ on his account".
Renderings of "τὰ ἄγη ἐλαύνειν προεῖπον ἕνεκα ἐκείνου":
(a) drive off the cattle/herds,
(b) drive out the goats (from a connection with αἴξ),
(c) drive out the pollutions (from ἄγος "pollution, cause of pollution").
Question 2: what does transmitted "τὰ ἄγη" mean in this sentence, and which rendering is correct? Is there a standard construal? Answer briefly.

----- ANTWORT/ANSWER (gpt-6-astra, effort=medium; 13.6k tokens) -----

1. **(iii)** is standard: **δέκατος αὐτός** means “himself as the tenth,” counting him with nine others—thus “one of the ten generals.” It does not count terms of office or years. The same idiom makes **πέμπτον αὐτὸν στρατηγόν** “himself one of five generals,” not “serving his fifth term”; that proposed parallel rests on a mistaken interpretation.

2. **(c)** is correct: **τὰ ἄγη** is the plural of **ἄγος**, “religious pollution” or “defilement.” The standard construal is “just as they had previously demanded that the pollutions be expelled, on his account.” **προεῖπον … ἐλαύνειν** expresses a prior demand, not an announcement that they themselves would drive something away.
