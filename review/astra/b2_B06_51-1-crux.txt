Thucydides 2.51.1, transmitted: "καὶ ἄλλο παρελύπει κατ’ ἐκεῖνον τὸν χρόνον οὐδὲν τῶν εἰωθότων· ὃ δὲ καὶ γένοιτο, ἐς τοῦτο ἐτελεύτα."

(the very year proved free of the other ailments; whatever else anyone was already suffering from, it all resolved into this disease).

Two printed construals of 2.51.1:
(A) English: "and at that season nothing of the wonted ailments was wanting besides, but whatever else arose, in this it came to its end." — face value of παρελύπει ("was omitted/lacking"): nothing of the customary was left out — the ordinary ailments all occurred too, and whatever did arise ended in the plague.
(B) Ukrainian/Hebrew: "no other customary ailment attached itself to them at that time / beside it, nothing of the customary stood" — alongside the plague no other ordinary disease was present (immunity), and whatever did chance to arise, it ended in this.

Questions:
(1) Between the face-value reading of παρελύπει (παραλείπω, "leave out") and the immunity reading, which is compatible with 2.49.1's ἄνοσον ("the year proved exceptionally free of the other ailments")?
(2) Does the transmitted ὃ δὲ καὶ γένοιτο, ἐς τοῦτο ἐτελεύτα support one reading more than the other?
(3) Which rendering of 2.51.1 should a translation print: "nothing of the wonted ailments was wanting" (all occurred), "nothing else of the wonted ailments supervened" (immunity), or another phrasing? Answer briefly.

1. **B fits 2.49.1**, provided “immunity” means exceptional freedom from other ailments, not absolute exclusion. A’s assertion that all customary ailments occurred conflicts with that description.

2. “Whatever did happen to arise ended in this” naturally qualifies B by allowing exceptions. Taken alone, however, the clause accommodates either reading; it does not settle the preceding verb’s meaning.

3. Print: **“No other customary ailment troubled them at that time; whatever did happen to arise ended in this disease.”** Crucially, παρελύπει is from παραλυπέω, “trouble besides,” not παραλείπω, “leave out,” whose imperfect would be παρέλειπε. Thus A’s claimed “face value” rests on a mistaken identification of the verb.
